What’s new

A demo with a made-up product. In Polylog, the language is picked automatically from each user’s browser. The demo translations were written by hand; if one sounds off in your language, tell us when you join.

How it works

  1. Write an update in MarkdownTag it New, Improved or Fixed. Write in any language you like.
  2. Review the translationsPolylog translates it into 30 languages right away. Every language is editable before you publish.
  3. Add one line to your siteThe widget is a small script. If our servers ever go down, your app keeps working; only the widget hides. <script src="https://cdn.polylog.example/w.js" data-project="acme" defer></script>

Why another changelog tool?

Because multilingual changelogs are priced for big companies. If your users are worldwide, they shouldn’t all get updates in English.

Questions

Is this live yet?

No. We’re checking that enough people want it before building. If you join, you’ll hear from us once, when early access opens.

Which languages?

30 at launch: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Czech, Romanian, Hungarian, Greek, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

How good are the translations?

They’re machine translations made with a large language model, and you can edit every language before publishing. Short product updates translate well; you stay in control.

Will the widget slow down my site?

It loads after your page and is kept small. It never blocks your app from working.

Can I leave anytime?

Yes. You can export all your updates, in every language, as Markdown files.
